## Service Overview

HydraKV is an embedded and clustered log-structured merge-tree (LSM) key-value engine written in Rust. It was built to explore lock-free concurrency and Raft-based consensus under high network partition strain.

### Architectural Decisions

1. **Raft over Multi-Paxos**: Raft was selected for its clean separation between leader election, log replication, and safety invariants. Leader election achieves convergence in `< 250ms` upon heartbeat loss.
2. **Zero-Copy Serialization with Cap'n Proto**: Rather than paying the JSON or Protocol Buffer deserialization penalty on critical read paths, HydraKV uses Cap'n Proto memory-mapped buffers directly from the page cache.
3. **Partition Tolerance**: Under Jepsen partition tests, split-brain write corruption was verified to be 0% with linearizable read consistency leases.

### Key Trade-Offs

- **Write Amplification vs Read Latency**: Compaction frequency was tuned to favor low p99 read latencies (< 15ms) at the expense of higher background disk I/O write amplification during bulk ingestion.
- **Strict Linearizability**: We traded off off-leader read availability in exchange for monotonic read guarantees.
